## Favorite Movie: *Four Sisters and a Wedding*

**Four Sisters and a Wedding** (2013) is a Filipino family comedy-drama directed by *Cathy Garcia-Molina*. It follows the Salazar sisters as they reunite to stop their youngest brother, CJ, from marrying a woman they barely know.

### The Salazar Siblings
* **Teddie** – The eldest sister working in Spain who feels pressured to appear successful.
* **Bobbie** – The overachieving corporate manager based in New York. **(my fav character)**
* **Alex** – The independent, free-spirited film director.
* **Gabbie** – The gentle, dutiful schoolteacher who stays home to care for their mother.
* **CJ** – The youngest and only brother, his sudden engagement sparks the family reunion.

### Favorite Quote
> *"Bakit parang kasalanan ko?"* — Bobbie Salazar
